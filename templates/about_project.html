Intelligent Academic Assistant for Saber 11

AI-Powered Academic Guidance, Assessment and Performance Prediction System for Saber 11 Students

What Is This Project?

Intelligent Academic Assistant for Saber 11 is an innovative educational platform designed to help students evaluate their academic performance, identify strengths and weaknesses, and estimate their potential results in the Saber 11 examination through Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques.

How Does It Work?

1

Students complete an academic assessment.

2

Socioeconomic information is collected.

3

Machine Learning models analyze the data.

4

Personalized predictions and recommendations are generated.

Target Audience

High School Students
Educational Institutions
Academic Advisors
Teachers

Educational Benefits

  • Identification of academic strengths and weaknesses.
  • Early prediction of possible Saber 11 performance.
  • Personalized academic guidance.
  • Improvement of study strategies and preparation plans.
  • Support for educational decision-making.
