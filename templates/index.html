Intelligent Academic Assistant for Saber 11

AI-Powered Academic Guidance, Assessment and Performance Prediction System for Saber 11 Students

Project Overview

Intelligent Academic Assistant for Saber 11 is an advanced educational platform that combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and educational analytics to support students during their preparation process for the Saber 11 examination.

The system evaluates academic knowledge, analyzes socioeconomic factors, predicts future performance and generates personalized recommendations that help students strengthen their preparation strategies and improve their academic outcomes.

Problem Statement

Many students take the Saber 11 examination without a clear understanding of their current academic performance or an estimation of their potential results. This lack of information often limits effective preparation strategies and reduces opportunities for targeted academic improvement.

Educational institutions also face challenges in identifying students who may require additional support before taking the examination, making early intervention difficult.

Project Justification

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning provide innovative opportunities to improve educational decision-making by analyzing large volumes of historical educational data and identifying patterns associated with student performance.

This project seeks to provide a practical academic support tool capable of helping students better understand their strengths, weaknesses and overall level of preparation before taking the Saber 11 examination.

General Objective

To develop an Intelligent Academic Assistant capable of evaluating academic knowledge, analyzing socioeconomic characteristics, estimating student performance and generating personalized recommendations through Machine Learning techniques.

Specific Objectives

  • Analyze historical Saber 11 educational data.
  • Prepare and preprocess academic information.
  • Train and evaluate Machine Learning models.
  • Compare Random Forest and Linear Regression models.
  • Evaluate model performance using R², MAE and RMSE.
  • Develop an interactive educational platform.
  • Provide performance predictions and recommendations.
  • Support educational decision-making processes.
